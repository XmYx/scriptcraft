# Gaps (ranked)

1. **PDF export** with Liberation Mono embedded, from the layout's pages (craft-libs `craft-pdf`).
2. **Accessibility depth**: caret and selection reported on the script nodes beyond egui's text
   fields, high-contrast theme check, a screen-reader pass (`needs screen reader test`).
3. **FDX export** (write what FDX import reads), then styles (bold/italic/underline runs) both ways.
4. **Editor polish**: caret movement across wrapped lines (↑/↓ on the first/last visual line),
   selection and copy/paste across elements, find and replace, spelling (craft-libs `craft-proof`).
5. **Shared shell**: craft-libs `craft-menu`, `craft-keymap`, `craft-settings` (recent files, page
   setup, language); ScriptCraft catalogs in craft-i18n.
6. **Dual dialogue side by side** in layout and preview.
7. **Revisions**: revision marks, colored pages, locked pages and scene numbers (A/B pages).
8. **Reports**: scene, location and character reports; a breakdown export for scheduling.
9. **Outline view**: sections and synopses as an index-card board (shared with BoardCraft).
10. **Packaging**: icons at all sizes, desktop file, installers; launcher listing.
